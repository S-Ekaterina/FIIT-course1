# Theoretical foundations of Computer Science

> [!TIP]
> Without theory and understanding, practice makes no sense!

&nbsp;&nbsp;Just like PPI, I absolutely loved this course because it was entirely focused on logic and diagrams. We covered a wide range of essential and fascinating topics, such as Deterministic and Non-Deterministic Finite Automata, Pushdown Automata, and others.  
  
&nbsp;&nbsp;Later on, we dove into abstract computational models, working with Turing Machines, RAM Machines, and Counter Machines. We even completed several practical projects along the way, which I found incredibly engaging and exciting to work on.
