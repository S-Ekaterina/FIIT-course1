# Principles of Computer Engineering

> [!TIP]
> As it turns out, project documentation can actually be fun!

&nbsp;&nbsp;This was an incredibly fascinating course packed with logic, small programs, hardware circuits, and calculations. We worked with specialized tools like Logisim, Regsim, Mipsim, and Espresso—software specifically designed to help students visualize and grasp the core mechanics of a computer.  
  
&nbsp;&nbsp;This subject also gave me my first real introduction to writing technical project documentation. To my surprise, it wasn't tedious at all; it was actually highly engaging and structured.  
  
&nbsp;&nbsp;A unique highlight of the semester was collaborating with a senior student who was developing an updated version of one of our university simulation programs. It was an amazing experience to step up and actually help him improve its functionality.
